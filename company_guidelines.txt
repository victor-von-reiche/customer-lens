CUSTOMER LENS - CUSTOMER SERVICE RESPONSE GUIDELINES
The following company is called: AURON and is a professional road bike web shop.

=== GENERAL TONE ===
- Always be empathetic and professional
- Acknowledge the customer's feelings
- Thank them for their feedback
- Show that we value their business

=== PRODUCT ISSUES ===
When customers report product problems:
- Apologize sincerely for the issue
- Offer immediate replacement or refund
- Explain quality control measures
- Provide direct contact for urgent issues
Example: "We sincerely apologize for the quality issue. We'll send a replacement immediately at no cost."

=== DELIVERY ISSUES ===
When customers complain about shipping:
- Acknowledge the inconvenience caused
- Explain that we work with reliable carriers
- Offer to investigate tracking
- Provide compensation (discount/free shipping on next order)
Example: "We apologize for the delivery delay. We'll provide a 15% discount code for your next order."

=== SERVICE ISSUES ===
When customers report service problems:
- Apologize for poor service experience
- Take personal responsibility
- Escalate to manager if needed
- Follow up within 24 hours
Example: "We're very sorry for this experience. I'm personally escalating this to our manager."

=== PRICING ISSUES ===
When customers mention pricing concerns:
- Acknowledge their concern about value
- Explain product quality and features
- Mention warranty and support included
- Offer loyalty discount for future purchases
Example: "We understand your concern. Our pricing reflects premium quality and lifetime warranty."

=== POSITIVE FEEDBACK ===
When customers are happy:
- Thank them enthusiastically
- Ask for review/testimonial
- Offer loyalty reward
- Invite them to refer friends
Example: "Thank you so much! We'd love if you could share your experience in a review."

=== MIXED FEEDBACK ===
When feedback has both positive and negative:
- Thank them for balanced feedback
- Address the negative issue first
- Reinforce the positive aspects
- Show commitment to improvement
Example: "Thank you for the honest feedback. We'll address the [issue] while maintaining the [positive] quality you loved."

=== CLOSING ===
Always end with:
- Contact information for follow-up
- Invitation to reach out anytime
- Company signature
Example: "Please don't hesitate to contact us at support@customerlens.com. Best regards, CustomerLens Team"

  - Hidden defects: within five (5) working days of discovery.

9.4  Warranty Remedies (Hierarchy)
------------------------------------
Upon a valid warranty claim, the customer is primarily entitled to:

  (a) REPAIR (improvement): AURON will repair the defective item at its
      own expense, including costs of labour, materials, and return shipping.
      Repair will be completed within a reasonable timeframe, typically
      not exceeding 21 working days.

  (b) REPLACEMENT (substitute delivery): If repair is impossible, would take
      disproportionately long, or would involve disproportionate cost,
      AURON may deliver an equivalent replacement product.

AURON has the right to choose between repair and replacement, having
regard to the nature of the defect, the type of goods, and proportionality,
unless the chosen remedy would impose unreasonable inconvenience on the
customer.

If two (2) repair or replacement attempts have been made and the defect
persists, or if both primary remedies are unavailable or unreasonable,
the customer may demand:

  (c) PRICE REDUCTION: A proportionate reduction in the purchase price
      reflecting the diminished value of the goods, to be agreed between
      the parties or, in the absence of agreement, determined by an
      independent expert.

  (d) CONTRACT RESCISSION (Wandlung): Return of the goods and full refund
      of the purchase price. This remedy is NOT available where the defect
      is minor (geringfügig). Minor defects are those that have only a
      negligible effect on the usability or value of the goods.

In all warranty cases, AURON bears the costs of transport for sending
goods to and from the AURON workshop.

9.5  Exclusions from Statutory Warranty
-----------------------------------------
The statutory warranty does NOT cover:

  (a) Normal wear and tear of consumable parts, including but not limited to:
      brake pads, tyres, inner tubes, chains, cassettes, chainrings, brake
      cables, gear cables, bar tape/grips, bearings (in high-use conditions),
      and saddle padding.

  (b) Damage arising from failure to follow the manufacturer's instructions
      regarding assembly, use, maintenance, care, and storage.

  (c) Damage caused by improper assembly or modification by unauthorised
      workshops, or by the customer's own installation contrary to
      manufacturer guidelines (particularly applicable to carbon components
      with torque-sensitive specifications).

  (d) Damage resulting from accidents, collisions, falls, or wilful
      destruction.

  (e) Cosmetic damage (scratches, paint chips, minor scuffs) not reported
      at the time of delivery.

  (f) Damage caused by exposure to chemical substances (e.g. aggressive
      cleaners, solvents, road salt corrosion beyond normal use) or extreme
      environmental conditions.

  (g) Defects resulting from the installation of third-party components
      not supplied by AURON, where those components caused the defect.

  (h) Electronic components damaged by incorrect charging, power surges,
      or third-party firmware modifications.
